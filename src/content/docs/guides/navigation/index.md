# Maps and Waymarkers

Use **Frontier's Map** to record a route and **Wilderlands Waymarkers** to make
that route visible when you travel through the world.

## Frontier's Map

1. Craft a **Frontier's Map**, right-click it, and give it a name.
2. Use the line, eraser, text, and stamp tools to record roads, landmarks, and
   expedition notes. Stamps can be scaled and rotated.
3. Craft a **Sextant** when you need a precise location. Hold right-click for
   a few seconds while aiming at a block or landmark, then import the saved
   measurement with the Sextant button in the lower-right of the map.
4. Export a map to share it, or import a map onto a new or existing map.

The installed Legacy Stamp Pack adds additional stamp choices for Frontier's
Map. Use the in-game handbook's **Frontier's Map Guide** for the basic controls.

## Wilderlands Waymarkers

- Place **cairns** along roads, at crossings, and near important turns. The
  small, medium, tall, and flat variants let you match the marker to the route.
- Place **buoys** on water routes. Use a **lantern buoy** where a lit marker is
  useful at night; a **bag buoy** provides an oiled-hide option.
- Use local stone for cairns and keep marker spacing consistent so the trail is
  easy to follow in both directions.

Waymarkers are physical trail signs, while the map preserves the route and its
notes. For current recipes and updates, see [Frontier's Map on ModDB](https://mods.vintagestory.at/show/mod/53436)
and [Wilderlands Waymarkers on ModDB](https://mods.vintagestory.at/show/mod/19736).
