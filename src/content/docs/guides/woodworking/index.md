The server uses **ImmersiveWoodworking** to turn raw logs into firewood, sticks,
planks, and support beams through dedicated stations. Use the in-game guide's
**Woodworking** overview for exact recipes and machine-part lists.

## From tree to materials

**Falling Tree** by SaltyWater makes felled trees behave as falling trees rather
than simply disappearing.

For manual processing:

- Put a log on a **Chopping Block** and use an axe. A log becomes two half-logs,
  half-logs become firewood, and firewood becomes sticks.
- Build two **Sawhorses** directly beside each other. Put a log on them and use
  a **Pit Saw** from the end to make planks.
- Strip the bark from the side with a **Bark Spud**, an axe with a hammer in
  the off-hand, or a knife. Saw the debarked log into support beams.

## Automate it

The powered **Sawmill** turns logs into planks and debarked logs into support
beams. The powered **Chopper** turns logs directly into firewood. Drive either
machine with an axle from a windmill or water wheel, then follow its handbook
entry for assembly and loading.

You can have more info if you click the **Woodworking** entry in the handbook.
