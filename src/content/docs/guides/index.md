# Guides

These guides follow the server's survival playstyles: gathering, building, exploration, industry, and the systems that connect them. Each guide separates the practical journey from details that belong in the in-game handbook.

## Available guides

- [Economy](./economy/): Mint coins with Coinage and buy or sell goods through Vinconomy player shops.
- [Farming with Art of Growing](./farming/): Grow crops, improve their varieties, and process grain and grass.
- [Fibercraft and Dressmakers](./fibercraft/): Spin fibers, weave cloth, and craft clothing.
- [Maps and Waymarkers](./navigation/): Record routes with Frontier's Map and mark them in the world.
- [Toolsmith Forging](./toolsmith/): Forge, assemble, hone, and reforge tools with Toolsmith.
- [Waterfall and Water Placement](./waterfall/): Plan waterworks around Waterfall's natural-source restriction.
- [Woodworking](./woodworking/): Process logs by hand or automate them with ImmersiveWoodworking.
- [Mining](./mining/): Prospect, extract, process, and smelt ore from the Stone Age into a steel-era mining operation.
