Art of Growing turns farming into a longer crop cycle with more growth stages,
crop-specific nutrients, and useful by-products. The Breeding Addon adds seven
varieties: **Wild**, **Small**, **Medium**, **Decent**, **Large**, **Hefty**, and
**Gigantic**.

## Improve your varieties

Begin with Wild seeds and plant the best seed varieties you find. A mature crop
can produce seed for the next variety, letting a well-managed field gradually
move toward Gigantic crops. Higher varieties are also more valuable as food, so
reserve them for meals or for expanding your best seed stock.

## Process the harvest

- **Vegetables:** Keep seedling or seed items for replanting; use the rest in
  meals, pickling, or other food recipes.
- **Grains:** Place a wet grain bundle on the ground with **Shift + right-click**
  and let it dry. Place the dry bundle again, then use a stick on it with
  **right-click** to thresh the grain from the straw.
- **Grass:** Mow grass with a scythe. Mown grass dries over time; rain and snow
  slow the process. A hayfork helps collect larger amounts.

The in-game guide's **Crops** tab is the best reference for an individual
crop's stages and the items it produces. 

For animal processing, see the short [Butchering](./butchering/) subguide. Use
the in-game handbook's **Butchering: Guide** and **Butchering: Info** entries
for the complete manual.

## Bees and crop yields

The server's apiary system, **From Golden Combs**, lets bees improve nearby
harvests. Keep hives in open space with flowers nearby. Hives with a **Decent**
or **Large** population build pollination charges over time. When a nearby
crop, berry bush, or fruit tree is broken, the hive spends a charge and boosts
the drops:

- **Skep:** +10%
- **Ceramic hive:** +20%
- **Langstroth hive:** +30%

This is a harvest-yield bonus, not a growth-speed bonus. Place a charged hive
near the field, orchard, or berry patch, and use the in-game **From Golden
Combs** handbook for hive construction and pollination details.
