# Waterfall and Water Placement

**Waterfall** changes the usual water-bucket expectation: you cannot place a
new water source just anywhere. Water must be manually spread from a naturally
generated liquid body.

## Plan around the source

- Build canals, farms, mills, and decorative waterfalls beside a natural lake,
  river, or ocean edge.
- Start the waterworks at that natural body and spread the water manually to
  the place you need it.
- If a bucket refuses to create water in a dry, isolated location, that is the
  mod working as intended. Move the build closer to a natural source instead
  of planning around free water placement.

This is a placement limitation, so choose the source and route before building
the rest of the project. For current behavior and updates, see [Waterfall on
ModDB](https://mods.vintagestory.at/show/mod/22639).
