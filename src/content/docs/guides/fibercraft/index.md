The basic textile route is **fiber → twine → cloth → clothing**. Immersive
Fibercraft supplies the spinning and weaving stations; Dressmakers adds more
clothing recipes.

## Quick route

1. Gather **Flax Fibers**. **Wool & More** fibers can also be spun when that
   mod is installed.
2. For portable spinning, craft a **Wooden Drop Spindle**. Hold fibers in
   your offhand and the spindle in your main hand. Hold right-click to spin,
   then **Sneak + right-click** to extract the twine.
3. For regular production, craft and place a **Spinning Wheel**. Sit at it,
   open its menu while seated, load fibers, and collect the finished twine.
4. Craft and place a **Flying Shuttle Loom**. Sit at it, open the weaving menu,
   load twine, and collect the cloth.
5. Use the cloth in Dressmakers recipes. Depending on the item, you may need a
   **sewing kit**, **needle**, **awl**, or colored thread from **Tailor’s
   Delight**. Some clothing is available to everyone; class clothing may
   require the **Clothier** trait or the relevant class.

## Use the in-game guide

Open the handbook entries **Crafting Mechanic: Spinning Wheel**, **Crafting
Mechanic: Flying Shuttle Loom**, **Crafting Mechanic: Drop Spindle**, and
**Pattern Weaving** for the complete station instructions and recipe details.
For Dressmakers, search for the clothing item in the handbook before gathering
materials; the recipe tells you which tailoring tool and cloth it accepts.

Dressmakers requires **Tailor’s Delight** and **Expanded Matter**. For current
versions and mod changes, see [Immersive Fibercraft on ModDB](https://mods.vintagestory.at/show/mod/34327)
and [Dressmakers on ModDB](https://mods.vintagestory.at/show/mod/12621).
